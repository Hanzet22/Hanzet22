# Rights, Attribution & Takedown Policy

This policy explains how to report possible copyright, licensing, attribution, or permission issues in repositories maintained by Hanzet22. It is a project-maintainer contact procedure, not legal advice and not a replacement for GitHub's formal policies.

## Submit a report

For private correspondence, email **hypergarudatkj@gmail.com** with the subject `Rights / Attribution Review`. Include:

1. The exact public repository URL and affected file path(s), commit(s), or asset URL(s).
2. Identification of the copyrighted work or material and, where possible, the original source URL.
3. A concise explanation of your ownership or authority to represent the rights holder.
4. The basis of the concern (for example, missing attribution, incompatible license, unauthorized asset use, or alleged copyright infringement).
5. The requested remedy: attribution correction, source clarification, replacement, removal, or another specific action.
6. A reply address so the maintainer can ask reasonable follow-up questions.

Do not send passwords, API keys, government identifiers, private user data, or unrelated sensitive information. Public issues are not suitable for confidential evidence.

## Review and possible outcomes

Reports are reviewed in good faith. Depending on the evidence and the applicable license, a response may include:

- correcting or adding attribution and license notices;
- replacing an asset or implementation with an authorized alternative;
- removing or disabling the disputed material while reviewing the claim;
- documenting provenance or permission where use is authorized; or
- explaining why the material appears to be covered by an applicable license or permission.

A request is not automatically accepted as legally valid. The maintainer may request clarification and will avoid making public accusations while a claim is being reviewed. No fixed response or remediation timeline is guaranteed.

## Formal GitHub copyright process

For formal copyright complaints about content hosted on GitHub, rights holders should review and, where appropriate, use [GitHub's DMCA Takedown Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy) and its official submission route. GitHub—not this profile README—administers that process. Counter-notice and restoration procedures, where applicable, are governed by GitHub's policy and relevant law.

Please make only accurate, good-faith reports. Do not submit false or misleading claims, impersonate a rights holder, or use a takedown request to resolve unrelated disputes.

## Scope and limitations

- This policy covers repositories and assets maintained by Hanzet22 to the extent they are under the maintainer's control.
- Third-party dependencies, model weights, datasets, hosted media, and upstream projects may have separate owners and license terms.
- An attribution entry does not itself prove permission to use an asset; each item's license and provenance must be checked.
- This policy does not waive any rights, defenses, or procedures available to any party.
